## Overview

**The Pianist** is a digital signal processing (DSP) pipeline written in Python that synthesizes a multi-octave musical sequence in the time domain, corrupts the signal with narrowband sinusoidal interference at randomized frequencies, and performs **automated frequency-domain spectral peak suppression** via the Fast Fourier Transform (FFT) and Inverse Fast Fourier Transform (IFFT).

### DSP Pipeline Stages

1. **Dual-Octave Harmonic Note Synthesis (`Song_Elements_Time` & `Note_Freq_Array`):**
   * Synthesizes a 24-note melody at a sampling rate of $f_s = 44.1\text{ kHz}$ over $T = 3\text{ s}$ ($N = 132,300$ samples).
   * Each musical note (`C, D, E, F, G, A, B`) is generated by superposing its fundamental 3rd-octave ($f_3$) and 4th-octave ($f_4$) harmonic sinusoids gated over its time window $[t_i,\; t_i + T_i)$:
     $$x_{\text{clean}}(t) = \sum_{i} \left[\sin(2\pi f_{3,i} t) + \sin(2\pi f_{4,i} t)\right] \cdot \mathbb{I}_{[t_i,\, t_i + T_i)}(t)$$
2. **Stochastic Narrowband Interference Injection:**
   * Injects two continuous-wave sinusoidal noise tones at randomly drawn frequencies $f_{n1}, f_{n2} \sim \mathcal{U}(0, 512\text{ Hz})$ spanning the entire duration $T$:
     $$x_{\text{noisy}}(t) = x_{\text{clean}}(t) + \sin(2\pi f_{n1} t) + \sin(2\pi f_{n2} t)$$
3. **Blind FFT Spectral Peak Detection & Hermitian-Symmetric Zeroing (`Remove`):**
   * Transforms $x_{\text{noisy}}[n]$ into the discrete frequency domain via $X[k] = \text{FFT}\{x_{\text{noisy}}[n]\}$.
   * Because the continuous-duration noise tones concentrate energy across all $N$ samples (whereas individual musical notes are temporally localized to $125\text{ ms}$ windows), the two noise frequencies dominate the single-sided magnitude spectrum $|X[k]|$ ($0 \le k < N/2$).
   * Iteratively identifies the two dominant spectral indices $k_1^*, k_2^*$ in the positive-frequency half-spectrum and zeroes both the positive and conjugate negative DFT bins ($X[k^*] = 0,\; X[-k^*] = 0$) to preserve Hermitian symmetry before reconstructing the real time-domain audio via $x_{\text{filtered}}[n] = \text{Re}\{\text{IFFT}(X[k])\}$.
4. **Real-Time Audio Playback & 6-Panel Time/Frequency Visualization:**
   * Streams normalized clean, noisy, and filtered waveforms through the system audio interface (`sounddevice`) and plots a $2 \times 3$ comparison grid of time-domain waveforms and single-sided amplitude spectra.
